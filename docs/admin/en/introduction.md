# Welcome to He4rt

He4rt is your central management platform for users, teams, roles, and system administration. This knowledge base is your go-to reference for everything you can do in the admin panel.

## How This Guide Is Organized

- **Getting Started** — Learn how to navigate the panel, understand the dashboard, and customize your profile
- **User Management** — Create and manage users, assign roles and permissions, and organize teams
- **System** — Review the activity log and the mails the platform sent

## Need Help?

The Users, Teams, and Roles pages have a **Help** button next to the page title. It opens the guide for that page. **Documentation** in the sidebar opens this guide at any time.
