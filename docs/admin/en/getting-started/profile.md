# Your Profile

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and select **Profile**. The page asks for your password
first when you have not confirmed it in the last three hours.

## Available Settings

### Account

Update your **name** and **email address**.

### Password

Change your password. Type your current password to confirm. A new password logs out your other
sessions.

### Connected Accounts

You connect GitHub, Google, Discord or Twitch in the app, on **Settings → Security**. Click **Open
security in the app** to go there. The admin login takes only an email and a password, so a user
without a password sets one there first.

### Two Factor Authentication

Shown when two factor is on in **System → Settings → Authentication**. Show or regenerate your
recovery codes, or turn two factor off. Two factor is required while the setting is on, so after you
turn it off, the next page asks you to set it up again.

### Passkeys

Shown when passkeys are on. Add a passkey from this device, or remove one. You log in to the app with
a passkey. The admin login still asks for your password.

### Preferences

Choose the **language** of the admin (English or Portuguese - Brazil) and its **accent color**. The
accent color is yours only and does not change the admin for other users.

### Browser Sessions

See the devices that are logged in to your account. **Log out other sessions** asks for your password
and logs out every device except this one.
