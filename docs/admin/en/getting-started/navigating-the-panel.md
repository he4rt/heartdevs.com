# Navigating the Panel

The admin panel is organized into sections accessible from the sidebar on the left side of the screen.

## Sidebar

The sidebar is your main navigation tool. It contains links to all sections of the platform, organized into groups. You can collapse the sidebar by clicking the toggle button at the top to give yourself more screen space.

## Navigation Groups

The sidebar has these groups:

- **Setup** — Users, Teams, and Roles & Permissions
- **System** — Activity log, Mails, and Documentation (this guide)

## Search

Each list (Users, Teams, Roles, Activity log, Mails) has its own search field above the table.

## Contextual Help

The Users, Teams, and Roles pages have a **Help** button next to the page title. It opens the guide for that page.

## Back to the App

Click **App** at the bottom of the sidebar to open the app that every user sees. In the app, the
same place shows **Admin** to a super admin, to come back here.

## Your Menu

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar to switch the theme, open your **Profile**, or log out.
